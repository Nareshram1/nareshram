3.Fuck-playstore - (F*#k playstore (nothing personal)
A personal showcase for my applications – because who needs gatekeepers when you have JavaScript?

Browse My Masterpieces (and maybe a few quirky experiments)
Why This Exists (Besides Pure Boredom):
Let's be real, paying the "developer tax" to Play Store and App Store just to test out your brilliant (or not-so-brilliant) ideas as an amateur dev? That's tougher than debugging a legacy codebase on a Friday afternoon. This project grants me, the humble developer, the ultimate control over my apps. No more begging for approval or deciphering cryptic rejection emails!

The Truth, The Whole Truth, and Nothing But The Truth:
Okay, so part of it was indeed getting bored of staring at my ceiling. But mostly, it's about freedom! Freedom to deploy a calculator app that only adds 2 and 2 (and still gets it wrong sometimes), or a social media app where the only user is me. Plus, building your own app store just sounds cool, right? It's like having your own digital lemonade stand, but with less sticky mess and more code.

Secure Distribution (Mostly)
Upload and distribute your apps securely with admin-only access. Share your creations with anyone while maintaining full control. (Disclaimer: "Secure" means I tried my best, don't go storing your secret cookie recipes here... yet.)
